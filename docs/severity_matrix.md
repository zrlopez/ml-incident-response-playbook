# Severity Matrix

## SEV-1

- Full outage or critical production failure.
- Large customer or business impact.
- Immediate escalation required.
- Executive visibility likely.

## SEV-2

- Major degradation with clear user or business impact.
- Short-term workaround may exist.
- Cross-functional coordination required.

## SEV-3

- Limited impact or partial degradation.
- No broad outage.
- Standard incident handling is usually enough.

## SEV-4

- Low impact issue.
- No immediate production risk.
- Track and fix in normal workflow.
